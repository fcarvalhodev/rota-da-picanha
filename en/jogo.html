The Cattle Cycle

Every candidate promises cheap picanha. But it is the cattle that set the price. On this ranch you are the breeder from 2022 to 2026: sell cows whenever you like and see what happens to the price.

How to play: each year, choose how many cows to sell and close the year. A cow sold is money now; a cow kept becomes a calf, and a steer two years later. The promise button is there for you to test.
